# User Stories & Tasks

Below are the core user stories derived from our Product Backlog, broken down into actionable tasks.

### Epic 1: Network Infrastructure
**User Story 1:** As a Khayelitsha resident, I want a reliable wireless connection so that I can access the internet without relying on expensive mobile data.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   Survey Installation Locations
    *   Fibre Network Setup
    *   Install Wireless Towers

### Epic 2: Customer Onboarding & Support
**User Story 2:** As a customer, I want an easy way to register and pay for the service so that I can get connected quickly.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   Conduct community registration drives
    *   Set up Customer Payment System
    *   Router Configuration
**User Story 3:** As a customer, I want reliable support so that if my internet goes down, it gets fixed fast.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   Establish Customer Support channels
    *   Set up Network Monitoring systems
    *   Collect Customer Feedback

### Epic 3: Product Delivery & Marketing
**User Story 4:** As a small business owner, I want a stable 10Mbps uncapped package so that I can run my daily operations smoothly.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   Install Wireless Equipment
    *   Provide the 10 Mbps Internet Package
    *   Launch Marketing Campaign

### Epic 4: Digital Presence
**User Story 5:** As a potential customer, I want to view your services online so that I can trust your business.
*   **Tasks:**
    *   UI/UX Design for the website
    *   Website Development
    *   Testing & Quality Assurance
    *   Review against mockups

## Reflection
 Working on the user stories completely changed the way I approach project planning. In the past, I would just make a to-do list of features to build, but I learned that Agile is about focusing on the value delivered to the customer. Translating our interview data into specific stories helped me step into the shoes of a Khayelitsha resident who just needs reliable internet for their child's schoolwork. My role was to collaborate with my team to break these broad stories into granular, actionable tasks on our Trello board. I learned that keeping tasks small and specific is the secret to avoiding team bottlenecks and ensuring everyone knows exactly what to work on every day. It made our massive goal feel completely manageable.
